If you’re thinking about a trip to Whitefish, consider this your sign to go. We arrived with high expectations, and Whitefish easily surpassed them. The scenery is the rare kind that’s both calming and jaw-dropping: quiet lakes, dramatic peaks, and big sky in every direction.  

We traveled with my husband’s whole family, 10 of us in total. The grandparents are in their 70s, the parents are 40-something, and the kids ranged from 4 to 12. If a destination can keep that group happy and well-fed, it’s doing something right. Here’s how we spent our week.

![](/assets/images/Montana.JPG)

## Day 1: Travel Day
**✈️ Getting There:** Flight: Nonstop on Alaska Airlines from San Diego to Kalispell

**🚗 Car Rental:** We used Avis, but any rental company works 

**🏨 Stay:** [The Lodget at Whitefish Lake]([https://www.visitpcv.com/](https://lodgeatwhitefishlake.com/?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=20902368618&gbraid=0AAAAAD5XAYRJJ2hwSHo6Q02I8DGOErHGq&gclid=CjwKCAjw_eLVBhBEEiwAeaYZfPk5WkWnzGdhPOG4b9NNBRGQszWhMp3rnXehSPKMYHcpJJetUhSP6hoC77UQAvD_BwE))
  - It’s a bit dated, but the location, family-friendly rooms and proximity to Glacier National Park made it the clear winner for a group our size

**Dinner:** At the hotel, followed by s’mores on the lawn and a rock-throwing session into the lake

![](/assets/images/Rockthrow.jpg)

## Day 2: Whitefish Mountain Resort
**Breakfast:** [Loula's Cafe](https://www.loulaswhitefish.com/)

**Morning:** [Alpine Slide at Whitefish Mountain Resort](https://skiwhitefish.com/alpine-slides/)
 - This isn't a big buzzing summer mountain resort. It's smaller, sleepier, and quieter

**Lunch:** [Tiki Bar](https://lodgeatwhitefishlake.com/tiki-bar-grill)

**Afternoon:** Pool 

**Dinner:** [Abruzzo](https://www.abruzzoitaliankitchen.com/)

**Dessert:** [Clydesdale Creamery](https://www.clydesdalecreamery.com/)
 - Order the specialty milkshake, if you dare

## Day 3: Downtown & Lake Life
**Breakfast:** [Montana Coffee Traders](https://coffeetraders.com/pages/downtown-whitefish?srsltid=AU7gw4UIiSxuCy7aMHwQfagcO49L2yPPR0OLyx3OkpFF2C9gn90hXGmz)

**Morning:** We strolled downtown for shopping and souvenires, then went back to the hotel to kayak on the lake
 -  Kayaks are free through the Lodge

**Lunch:** Homemade in our room

**Afternoon:** You guessed it...pool 

**Dinner:** [Farmer’s Market](https://whitefishfarmersmarket.org/) at Depot Park, with food trucks, live music, and plenty of room for the kids to roam

## Day 4: Glacier National Park
**Breakfast:** In our room

**Morning:** [Hidden Lake Overlook Trail](https://www.nps.gov/places/hidden-lake-trailhead.htm)
 -  We were on the road by 5:30 AM. Not ideal, but it paid off: Logan Pass had plenty of parking when we pulled in at 7:30 AM 
 -  We never planned to hike the whole trail. Our goal was to wander and take some photos. Somewhere along the way, “wandering” turned into the ENTIRE hike. Kids who had never walked more than one mile finished a 2.7-mile roundtrip without a single complaint
 -  The early start provided perfect hiking weather. It wasn’t too cold and wasn’t yet too hot
 -  On the drive back, we stopped at Lake McDonald to dip our toes, skip rocks, and soak in the famous rainbow pebbles

![](/assets/images/GlacierHike.jpg)
*📍 Hidden Lake Overlook Trail*

**Lunch:** [Freda's](https://www.glacierparkcollection.com/lodging/west-glacier-village/dining/)
 -  Not recommended; food was very meh. It wasn’t part of the plan, but hungry stomachs don’t negotiate

**Afternoon:** Naps all around, then...the pool 

**Dinner:** [Boat Club Bar](https://lodgeatwhitefishlake.com/eat-drink?utm_source=googlemybusiness)

## Day 5: Wakeboarding & Rodeo
**Breakfast:** In our room

**Morning:** We rented a boat for four hours and spent it tubing, wakeboarding, and wake surfing on Whitefish Lake

**Lunch:** [Tiki Bar](https://lodgeatwhitefishlake.com/tiki-bar-grill)

**Afternoon:** Pool. Are you sensing a theme?

**Dinner:** In our room

**Evening:** [Brash Rodeo](https://glaciercountryrodeo.com/)
 -  Buy tickets ahead and arrive early for good seats
 -  Bull riding, barrel racing, and cowboy hats - it was peak Montana.

## Got Older Kids?
If your kids are older or more adventurous, Whitefish has plenty more to offer:
 -  [Aerial Adventure Park at Whitefish Mountain Resort](https://skiwhitefish.com/aerial-adventure-park/)
 -  Mountain Biking
 -  White water rafting
 -  Ziplining
 -  Hot air balloon rides

## If We Did Our Trip Again...
**More Breweries.** Our schedule and their hours never quite lined up, but these are on my list:
 - [Backslope Brewing](https://backslopebrewing.com/), conveniently located on the way back from Glacier
 - [Bonsai Brewing](https://bonsaibrewery.com/) (closed Mondays)
 - [Blackstar BrewPub](https://www.blackstarmt.com/)
 - [Thirty Eight](https://thirtyeightwhitefish.com/), an open-air spot downtown with live music and food trucks

**Bulldog Saloon.** It’s been around since 1903, and its URL is fart-slobber.com. How can you not visit?! Missing this one still stings.

**More time in Glacier.** Next time we’ll spend an afternoon picnicking and swimming at Lake McDonald and strolling Apgar Village. This time, we simply didn’t pack enough food for our hungry crew. 

## Final Thoughts
Whitefish was one of those rare trips that worked for everyone, from the 4-year-old to the 75-year-old. We spent our days on mountains, lakes, and trails, with some very serious pool time in between. Ten people, three generations, zero complaints. Whitefish, you nailed it.
